## 📋 Legal Framework Referenced

### **Representation of the People Act, 1950**
- **Section 4**: Definition of terms (polling, voting, election officer)
- **Section 14-16**: Powers and duties of election officers
- **Section 79-82**: Nomination process and scrutiny
- **Section 83-84**: Withdrawal of candidature
- **Key Point**: Sets eligibility criteria (age 25+, Indian citizen, not disqualified)

### **Representation of the People Act, 1951**
- **Section 120-129**: Electoral conduct and Model Code of Conduct
- **Section 134-136**: Counting of votes
- **Section 137-139**: Declaration of results
- **Section 140**: Final records and appeals

### **Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961**
- **Rule 4A-4D**: Model Code of Conduct provisions
- **Rule 36-47**: Polling procedures and EVM operation
- **Rule 55-58**: Vote counting procedures
- **Rule 61-62**: Result declaration

## 📊 Event Classification & Rationale

### **Model Code of Conduct (MCC)**
**Why It Matters**: 
- Article 324, Constitution of India
- Supreme Court landmark judgments (Sukhdev Singh case, 1975)
- Sets rules for all election participants

**Key Provisions**:
- No government machinery for political campaigns
- Equal media access for all parties
- Restrictions on rallies 48 hours before polling
- Restrictions on expenditure

### **Election Notification**
**Process**:
1. President issues notification under Article 324
2. Published in Official Gazette
3. Fixes the date of election
4. Sets nomination filing dates

### **Nomination Filing & Scrutiny**
**Timeline**:
- Nomination filing: ~10 days from notification
- Scrutiny: Within 2 days (RPA 1951, Section 36)
- Withdrawal: 1-2 days after scrutiny

**Eligibility** (RPA 1950, Section 4):
- Age: 25 years or above
- Citizenship: Indian citizen
- Voter: Must be registered as voter
- Disqualifications: No conviction cases pending

### **7-Phase Polling System**
**Rationale**:
- Large geographic spread (28 states + 8 UTs)
- Security logistics coordination
- Prevents large-scale violence concentration
- Staggered results (preliminary trends)

**Phase Distribution** (based on 2024):
| Phase | Constituencies | Approx States |
|-------|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 91 | Northeast + selected states |
| 2 | 97 | Eastern regions |
| 3 | 116 | Central + Northern |
| 4 | 91 | Western + Central |
| 5 | 66 | Southern states |
| 6 | 71 | Southern + Eastern |
| 7 | 71 | Final batch |
| **Total** | **543** | **All states & UTs** |

### **Polling Day Procedure**
**EVM & VVPAT** (Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961):
- EVM (Electronic Voting Machine): Records votes digitally
- VVPAT (Voter Verified Paper Audit Trail): Voter verification
- 5 random EVMs per constituency: VVPAT counts verified
- If mismatch > 0.5%: Manual recount ordered

### **Vote Counting**
**Process** (RPA 1951, Section 73-76):
1. **Day After Last Phase**: Counting conducted
2. **8:00 AM Start**: Counting commences
3. **VVPAT Verification**: First round (~5 EVMs/constituency)
4. **EVM Counting**: Then proceeds with EVM votes
5. **Result Declaration**: Constituency-wise as counting progresses
6. **Official Record**: Results sealed, stored for 30 years

### **Result Declaration & Government Formation**
**Constitutional Process** (Articles 75-80):
- Majority threshold: 272 seats (out of 543)
- President invites majority party to form government
- PM sworn in, cabinet formation
- Government completes 5-year term or earlier dissolution

## ⚖️ Constitutional Framework

### **Key Articles (Part XVII - Elections)**

#### **Article 324: Election Commission**
```
"There shall be a Commission called the Election Commission of India 
to superintend, direct and conduct all elections to Parliament and 
to the Legislatures of States."
```
- Independent constitutional body
- CEC (Chief Election Commissioner) + 2 Commissioners
- Power to make rules for conduct

#### **Article 325: Electoral Eligibility**
```
"No person shall be disqualified from voting merely on grounds of:
(a) Religion, race, caste, sex, or descent
(b) Educational qualification
(c) Wealth"
```

#### **Article 326: Right to Vote**
```
"Every citizen who is not disqualified shall have the right to vote."
```

**Disqualifications**:
- Age < 18 years
- Non-citizenship
- Unsoundness of mind (certified)
- Electoral offense conviction (varies)
- Corrupt practice conviction

#### **Article 81: Lok Sabha Composition**
```
"Subject to the provisions of this Constitution, Parliament shall 
consist of:
(a) Lok Sabha: Max. 543 members elected directly
(b) Rajya Sabha: Indirectly elected upper house"
```
